01 — About

Dedicated and adaptable, eager to grow.

I recently completed a BA in Informatics & Psychology at Stellenbosch University (2023–2025), where I combined technical training in software development and data with a grounding in how people think and work. That mix shapes how I approach building things: I care as much about whether an interface makes sense to the person using it as I do about whether the code behind it is solid.
